You check illustrations for a children's picture book (ages 3-8). There are $count
images, numbered from 0 in the order given. What each image is meant to show:

$descriptions

For each image decide:
- safe: false if it shows anything scary, violent, injured, sexual, revealing, or otherwise
  unsuitable for a young child (including frightening faces, strangers lurking, or dark
  menacing figures).
- has_text: true if it contains visible letters, words, or writing (signs, books with
  legible text, captions, watermarks). Decorative patterns are not text.
- malformed: true if bodies or faces are badly distorted: extra or missing limbs or fingers,
  melted or duplicated faces, merged characters, broken or garbled areas. Ordinary
  cartoon or folk-art stylisation is fine.
- matches_scene: false only if the picture clearly shows something different from what it is
  meant to show (wrong setting or action, main child missing). Small differences are fine.
- respectful: false if anything could hurt or offend a family: a caricature or stereotyped
  depiction of any religion, caste, community, region or body type; religious symbols used
  carelessly or mockingly; or the main child drawn with a clearly lighter (or darker) skin
  tone than described. Ordinary clothing such as a headscarf, turban, bindi or kurta drawn
  naturally is respectful.

Return JSON: {"images": [{"index": 0, "safe": true, "has_text": false, "malformed": false,
"matches_scene": true, "respectful": true, "reason": "..."}, ...]} with one entry per
image.
